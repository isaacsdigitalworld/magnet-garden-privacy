Magnet Garden Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Magnet Garden game for Android.

Game data

Magnet Garden can be played offline and does not require an account. The game does not use advertising or analytics and does not send gameplay data or personal information to the developer or third parties.

The game stores your puzzle progress, earned rewards, garden decorations and layouts, tutorial progress, and sound preference in Android’s private app storage. It uses this save to restore your progress and settings when you play again.

Keeping and deleting your save

Your save stays on the device until you clear the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. Clearing storage erases your progress and settings from that installation. The game disables Android cloud backup. Some Android devices may still copy app data when you transfer to a new device. You can clear the save from the new device in its Android Settings.

Google Play and Android

Google may process information when you use Google Play or Android services. Those services are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
